Jeopardy! Buzz-in vs Coryat Analysis Tool
Overview
Interactive scatter plot visualizing the relationship between buzz-in percentage and estimated Coryat scores for Jeopardy! contestants. Built with React and Recharts.
Key Components
Data Generation:

100+ synthetic data points across 4 skill tiers (weak, average, strong, elite)
10 named players (champions + benchmarks) with historical stats
Jittered coordinates (±1.5% accuracy, ±2% buzz-in) to prevent artificial gridding

Critical Constraints:

Buzz-in range: 10-60% (NOT higher - with 3 players, >50% is unrealistic)
Weak players (<65% accuracy) capped at 35% buzz-in
Elite players (>85% accuracy) must buzz ≥25%
